  1. Free and non-profit. {{NAME}} is a free guidance tool, not a business. We are not affiliated with the government (CICC, NTC, BSP, DICT, PNP, SEC), GCash, Maya, or any bank, courier or company.
  2. Guidance only. Results are estimates and guidance only. They are not legal, financial or professional advice and there is no guarantee of accuracy. The tool can be wrong: some scams are missed and some genuine messages are flagged. “No red flags found” does not mean a message is safe.
  3. You are responsible for your decisions. Before you pay, send money, or click, verify through the official app or hotline and ask family, friends, or people you trust. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss or damage arising from use of the results.
  4. Do not submit sensitive personal data. Phone numbers, emails, card/account numbers, OTP codes, names and addresses are masked on your device before sending, but masking is not perfect. Blank out anything else (passwords, ID numbers, other people’s addresses, etc.) before pasting. Do not paste other people’s messages if you are not allowed to share them.
  5. What is sent. The rules check runs on your phone only. When AI is ON, a masked copy of the message (and of the sender field, if filled in, also masked) is sent to our server, which forwards it to {{AI_DISPLAY_NAME}}. {{AI_PRIVACY_EN}} You can turn AI off at any time. See Privacy.
  6. What we keep. We do not store or log the text (including the masked copy). The server writes a log line without message content: request ID, route, status, latency, risk band, AI status, AI model name and error code. It also keeps anonymous daily counters (number of checks, AI calls, tokens and neurons used). Your IP address is used for rate limiting only as a one-way hash held in memory, dropped after about an hour of inactivity and never written to disk. In your browser only your language, AI on/off and notice acknowledgment are saved (localStorage). Like any website, hosting or network providers may keep ordinary technical logs (e.g. IP address and time) outside our control; these do not contain your text.
  7. Daily limit. Because the AI runs on a free allowance, there is a daily limit (it resets every day). When it is reached, or when there are too many requests, the result becomes rules-only (simpler) or AI pauses. AI features may change or disappear at any time, and the service is provided “as is” with no promise that it is always available.
  8. No abuse. Do not spam or automate large numbers of requests, bypass limits or protections, attack or hack the service, try to instruct or trick the AI (prompt injection), or use the service to deceive, harass or break the law. We may block such use.
  9. Changes. We may change these terms. The new version will be posted on this page; continued use means you accept it. Last updated: 2026-10-29.
  10. Reporting scams. This is not a reporting channel. If you were scammed or received a scam, call the CICC Inter-Agency Response Center hotline 1326 (24/7, toll-free; cicc.gov.ph/report), report to the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group (acg.pnp.gov.ph), and to your own bank or e-wallet’s official support (e.g. GCash, Maya) through their app or official hotline/website.
  11. No personal support. There is no personal contact or customer support for this service, and we cannot answer individual cases. For bug reports or feedback, use {{FEEDBACK_EN}} (public). Do not put personal details or message contents in a public report.
