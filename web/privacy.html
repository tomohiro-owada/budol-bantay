Taglish

  • Ang rules check ay sa phone mo lang ginagawa. Hindi ito umaalis sa device mo.
  • Kapag ON ang AI, isang masked na kopya ng mensahe ang ipinapadala sa aming server ({{API_ORIGIN}} API). Pinapalitan muna sa phone mo ang mga phone number, email, card/account number, OTP code, pangalan at address ng mga token tulad ng [PHONE] bago ipadala.

  • Maaari mong i-OFF ang AI gamit ang switch sa pangunahing pahina. Rules-only ang resulta kapag naka-OFF.
  • Takpan ang iba pang personal na detalye bago mag-paste.
  • Hindi kami konektado sa gobyerno (CICC, NTC, BSP, DICT, PNP, SEC), sa GCash, Maya, o sa anumang bangko, courier o kumpanya. Gabay lang ito, hindi legal o financial advice.

English

  • The rules check happens on your phone. It never leaves your device.
  • When AI is ON, a masked copy of the message is sent to our server ({{API_ORIGIN}} API). Phone numbers, emails, card/account numbers, OTP codes, names and addresses are replaced on your phone with tokens like [PHONE] before anything is sent.
  • Our server forwards the masked text to the AI provider ({{AI_DISPLAY_NAME}}) for the explanation. {{AI_PRIVACY_EN}}
  • We do not store or log the text — not even the masked copy. We only count anonymous daily counters (e.g. how many checks per day).
  • You can turn AI off with the switch on the main page. The result is rules-only when it is off.
  • Blank out any other personal details before pasting.
  • Not affiliated with the government (CICC, NTC, BSP, DICT, PNP, SEC), GCash, Maya, or any bank, courier or company. Guidance only, not legal or financial advice.
